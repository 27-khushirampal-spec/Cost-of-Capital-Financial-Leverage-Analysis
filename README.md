# Cost of Capital & Financial Leverage Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

A comparative financial analysis of selected Indian hospital and healthcare companies, focusing on **Cost of Capital, Capital Structure and Financial Risk**.

The study evaluates six companies over FY2023–FY2025 using **WACC, Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL), Degree of Financial Leverage (DFL) and Degree of Total Leverage (DTL)**.

## 🎯 Project Objective

- Calculate and compare WACC across selected healthcare companies
- Analyse capital structure using DOL, DFL and DTL
- Evaluate operating and financial risk
- Compare cost of capital and risk levels across companies
- Identify companies with relatively efficient capital structures
- Derive investment and managerial insights

## 🏥 Companies Covered

- Apollo Hospitals Enterprise Ltd.
- Max Healthcare Institute Ltd.
- Fortis Healthcare
- Narayana Hrudayalaya Ltd.
- Global Health Ltd. (Medanta)
- Aster DM Healthcare Ltd.

## 📊 Key Areas of Analysis

### Cost of Capital

WACC was calculated using:

- Cost of Equity (CAPM)
- Cost of Debt
- Debt and Equity Weights
- Tax Adjustment

### Financial Leverage

The project analyses:

- **DOL** – Operating Risk
- **DFL** – Financial Risk
- **DTL** – Overall Leverage Risk

### Comparative Analysis

Companies were compared based on:

- Average WACC
- DOL
- DFL
- DTL
- EPS
- Overall financial risk and capital efficiency

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Narayana Hrudayalaya** recorded the lowest average WACC at **3.87%**.
- **Fortis Healthcare** recorded the highest average WACC at **22.81%**.
- **Max Healthcare** had the lowest average DTL, indicating relatively lower overall leverage risk.
- **Apollo Hospitals** recorded the highest average EPS at **₹73.67**.
- Apollo Hospitals was identified as the **top investment pick** based on the project's comparative analysis.

## 📈 Investment Analysis

The study identified Apollo Hospitals as the preferred investment opportunity among the companies analysed, based on its earnings performance, revenue growth, moderate WACC and business strength.
